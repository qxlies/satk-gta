Third-party notice: gta-scout model descriptions
=================================================

What: text descriptions of GTA: San Andreas models (fields "description", "tags",
"confidence") from the gta-scout shared annotation pack, imported by
`satk describe import` into the local notes database (work/notes.sqlite, author
"import:gta-scout", tag "desc") and, if notes are exported, into data/notes/*.jsonl.

Source: https://github.com/Dryxio/gta-scout
        data/annotations/sa-2026-10-03.json (format gta-scout-annotations-v1,
        7,815 entries, pack sha256 ff923fae1836b66190521e0d0a38a599cc84a6b6136e33eb7bdbd0ab75272f94),
        revision ab2c6f27fd03ffcb934cabfa72b3e151f4273f13 (2026-10-03).
Changes: only the English part of FR/EN texts is kept by default (--text full keeps
        the original); entries are bound to models only when the local DFF bytes have the
        SHA-256 published in the pack. No gta-scout code is included in satk; the pack
        digest format (canonical JSON, SHA-256) is reimplemented from its description.
Note from the publisher: the descriptions were written and reviewed by AI agents from
        rendered images; they are observations, not verified facts.
